Privacy Policy

IS EASY CRM — customer messaging & order management

Last updated: 19 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how IS EASY CRM ("the Application", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data. The Application is an internal customer-relationship management tool operated by the owner of the Ultera online store ("the Store", the data controller) to receive and reply to customer messages and to manage orders. We respect your privacy and handle personal data in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

1. Information we collect

When you contact the Store through Facebook Messenger or Instagram, and when you place an order, we may collect:

  • Profile data from Meta: your name, profile picture, and page-/app-scoped user ID provided by Meta when you message our Page or account.
  • Message content: the text, images, and timestamps of messages you exchange with the Store.
  • Order & contact data you provide: phone number, recipient name, delivery city and post office/branch, and order details.

2. How we use your data

  • To receive, read, and respond to your messages and support requests.
  • To process, fulfil, and deliver your orders, and to arrange shipping and payment.
  • To keep a history of our conversation so we can provide consistent customer service.

We do not use your data for advertising profiling and we do not sell your personal data.

3. Legal basis for processing

We process your data to take steps at your request and to perform a contract with you (handling your enquiry and order), and on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing customer support. Where required, we rely on your consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

4. Service providers (data processors)

We use the following providers, who process data on our behalf and under contract:

  • Supabase Inc. — database hosting and storage (EU/Switzerland region). supabase.com/privacy
  • Vercel Inc. — application and webhook hosting (United States). vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Meta Platforms — delivery of messages via Messenger and Instagram. facebook.com/privacy/policy

To fulfil orders, delivery and payment details may also be shared with the delivery carrier (e.g. Nova Poshta) and the payment provider you choose, only to the extent needed to complete your order.

5. Data retention

We keep conversation and order data only for as long as necessary to provide customer service and to meet legal, accounting, and tax obligations. When data is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymised.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to access, correct, export, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to request its deletion. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. How to request data deletion

To have your personal data deleted, send an email to ismagilov.ultera@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request", from the account associated with your messages, or include the name you used to contact us. We will delete your data from our systems within 30 days and confirm by email.

8. Data security

Data is stored on access-controlled, encrypted infrastructure provided by the service providers listed above. Access is limited to the Store's authorised staff. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

9. Children

The Application is intended for customers of legal age and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest version.
